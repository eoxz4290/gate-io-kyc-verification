## What to have ready before you open the form

You'll move through this faster if the documents are already sitting on your desktop rather than being hunted down mid-flow.

- A valid, unexpired government ID. Passports, national identity cards and driver's licences are the usual accepted set, but which of the three you can use depends on the issuing country.
- Photo files in jpg or png format, larger than 10KB and no bigger than 4MB.
- Clean images. No watermarks, no stains, no glare, no cropped edges. If you're using a national ID card, both sides usually need to be visible.
- Your details typed exactly as they appear on the document. Spelling, date format, name order. This matters more than it sounds, because a mismatch is one of the three rejection reasons Gate lists.
- A browser that behaves. Gate's own troubleshooting advice names Chrome and Safari, and says a hung upload is usually fixed by refreshing or switching browsers.

Prepare the documents first, the lighting second. Read the list of Gate's published rejection reasons and you'll notice two of the three are photography problems and one is a typing problem:

1. Blurry or incomplete document photos
2. Entered information that doesn't match the document
3. A failed facial recognition check

None of those are about your paperwork being insufficient. They're about how you photographed it.

## The web flow, step by step

The desktop process as Gate currently documents it:

1. Log in and click your avatar in the top right.
2. Go to **Identity → Individual / Corporate Verification**, then click the verify button.
3. Enter nationality, country of residence, legal name, ID type and ID number. Check it before continuing, because the country you select can't be edited afterwards.
4. Move to the identity verification page. This is where you choose how to run the document and face steps.
5. Upload the front and back of your ID where asked.
6. Complete the face check, either in the browser or by switching to your phone.
7. Submit and wait.
8. Optionally, come back and complete address verification for higher limits.

One habit worth keeping: don't refresh the page while the flow is running. It's the fastest way to end up submitting half a verification.

## Three ways to clear the face check

Most guides mention one. Gate's page lists three, and the third is the one you'll want to know about in advance rather than discover after four failed attempts.

**Scan a QR code with the Gate app.** The document and face steps run on your phone, which usually has the better camera.

**Use your computer's webcam.** Works fine if the lighting is decent and the camera is clean.

**Fall back to a gesture photo.** If facial recognition won't pass, you can upload a photo of yourself holding your ID alongside the last four digits of your Gate UID. Same technical rules apply: jpg or png, over 10KB and under 4MB, gesture clearly visible, upper body in frame, nothing obscuring the document.

There's one regional footnote here. Users in mainland China go through video verification, while users outside it can upload the photo directly on the web. If the page throws "KYC FAILED: please try again in 5 minutes" or tells you to verify in person, that's usually an older ID document confusing the automated check. Open a support ticket with your real-name details and let a human look at it.

## Address verification: optional, and it moves your ceiling

You can stop after the identity step and use the account normally. Address verification exists for people who need more headroom.

It asks for your residential address plus a supporting document, such as a utility bill or bank statement. Once submitted, the status sits at "under review" and you wait. Nothing about this step is complicated, but it's also not the step that unlocks the account. Identity verification does that.

## Where it breaks, and how long you'll actually wait

The published review windows don't all match, which tells you something useful about how automated the front end is and how manual the back end isn't.

Gate's web verification guide says documents are generally reviewed within 48 hours, with longer times in individual cases. The FAQ material quotes up to around one business day for the automated path. Gate EU documentation puts the ceiling at 48 hours. If your selfie doesn't match your document, the case goes to manual review, and that's when the timeline stretches into several days rather than hours.

What you can control:

- Attempt limit: up to 10 verification attempts per day. If you've burned 10 within a 24-hour window, wait out the next 24 hours before trying again.
- If rejected, read the stated reason before resubmitting. Retrying the same blurry photo ten times just spends your attempts.
- If the flow hangs, try another browser before concluding anything is wrong with the account.

## When KYC isn't the last step you'll take

A verified account isn't a master key. Several Gate products run their own checks on top of it, and this is where people who assume "KYC done, I'm clear" get surprised.

**Gate Card.** Applications need Level 2 verification plus the identity documents specified for your region, and some card types also require proof of address issued within three months. Card eligibility is geographic, and completing KYC doesn't automatically make you eligible.

**Stocks and TradFi products.** These need a registered account at the right KYC level plus regional market eligibility. An account already trading crypto doesn't automatically carry over.

**Business accounts.** Companies go through KYB rather than individual KYC. That means company registration documents, ownership structure, director and representative documents, and ultimate beneficial owner details.

Other triggers for extra checks include fiat transactions, changes to your account information, and enhanced due diligence on accounts the risk engine flags. Gate's documentation also notes that a risk-flagged withdrawal can require additional KYC review before it clears.

## Quick answers

**Can I deposit or trade on Gate without completing KYC?**
No. Verification is required before deposits, withdrawals and trading, so an unverified account can't fund itself or move funds out.

**How long does Gate KYC take?**
The web guide quotes up to 48 hours, other Gate pages quote up to one business day, and EEA documentation says up to 48 hours. Cases that need manual review after a selfie mismatch take several days.

**Why was my Gate KYC rejected?**
Gate lists three reasons: blurry or incomplete document photos, information that doesn't match the document, and a failed facial recognition check. Fix the stated reason before resubmitting, and remember the daily cap is 10 attempts.

**Do I have to complete address verification?**
No, it's optional. Identity verification is what unlocks deposits, trading and withdrawals. Address verification raises your account limits.

**Should I verify before or after depositing?**
Before. There's no order in which an unverified account can receive funds, so starting the verification as soon as you register saves you a stuck first deposit.
